2. Resumen del curso 
The A2.1 Waystage 1 English course is designed to help students improve their basic English skills and build confidence in 
communicating in everyday situations. Through interactive and practical activities, students will learn to handle simple 
situations, talk about familiar topics, and understand short texts. 
3. Competencias de formación 
Competencia Resultado de aprendizaje esperado 
Understands very basic and frequently 
used phrases and expressions related to 
areas of immediate relevance, such as 
personal information, family, shopping, 
or work, demonstrating listening and 
reading comprehension skills in simple 
tasks that require identifying and 
recognizing concrete and predictable 
information. 
Understands short and simple sentences about everyday topics, such as 
personal or family information, when spoken slowly and clearly. 
Identifies specific information in brief and simple texts, such as 
advertisements, menus, or signs, on familiar or everyday topics. 
Participates in basic exchanges using simple phrases and expressions to ask 
and answer about immediate needs, such as asking for directions or buying 
something. 
Writes short and simple messages, like notes or postcards, describing 
personal situations or everyday aspects, such as a self-introduction or a basic 
request.
