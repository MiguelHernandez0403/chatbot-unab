2. Resumen del curso 
The B1.2 Threshold 2 English course is designed to help students consolidate and expand their intermediate English skills, 
with a focus on developing advanced linguistic competencies to manage an even wider range of communicative situations. 
Through dynami c activities and more complex linguistic challenges, students will learn to handle more demanding 
communicative contexts, participate more effectively in debates on broad and in -depth topics, and comprehend more 
complex and specialized texts. This course emphasizes refining fluency and accuracy in communication, enhancing students' 
ability to express ideas clearly and with nuance, and interact with greater confidence and competence across a diverse range 
of situations and topics in English.
