2. Resumen del curso 
The B1.1 Threshold 1 English course is designed to help students consolidate and expand their intermediate English  
skills, developing their ability to handle a wider range of communicative situations. Through interactive activities and  
linguistic challenges, students will learn to navigate more complex scenarios, engage in discussions on various topics,  
and analyze more detailed texts. This course focuses on refining fluency and accuracy in communication, strengthening  
students' ability to interact with greater confidence and effectiveness in diverse contexts and in exchanging more  
sophisticated ideas in English.
