2. Resumen del curso 
The English course B2.1 Vantage 1 is designed to help students refine and expand their advanced intermediate English skills, 
with a focus on consolidating higher-level language competencies to handle an even more complex variety of communicative 
situations. Through advanced activities and more extensive linguistic challenges, students will develop the ability to tackle 
sophisticated communicative contexts, participate effectively in detailed debates on specialized topics, and comprehend 
complex texts with g reater depth. This course emphasizes improving fluency and accuracy in communication, enabling 
students to express ideas with greater clarity and nuance, and interact confidently and competently in a wide range of 
situations and topics in English.
