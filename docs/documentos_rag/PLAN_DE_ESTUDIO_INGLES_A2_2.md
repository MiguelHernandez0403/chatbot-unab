2. Resumen del curso 
The A2.2 Waystage 2 English course is designed to help students consolidate and expand their basic English skills, as well as  
boost their confidence in communicating in a variety of everyday situations. Through dynamic and practical activities, 
students will learn to tackle more complex situations, discuss topics of personal interest, and understand more elaborate 
texts. This course focuses on improving fluency and accuracy in communication, strengthening students' ability to interact 
with greater confidence and effectiveness in English.
