2. Resumen del curso 
The English course B2.2 Vantage 2 is designed to take students with an upper -intermediate level to a more advanced  
mastery of the language, honing their linguistic skills to handle more complex and sophisticated communicative  
situations. This course focuses on perfecting fluency, accuracy, and expressive richness, preparing students to engage in  
detailed discussions, critical analyses, and the production of specialized texts. Through interactive activities and  
advanced linguistic challenges, students will develop a deeper and more nuanced understanding of English, learning to  
navigate both formal and informal registers with confidence. Additionally, the course fosters students' ability to  
interpret and use language precisely and appropriately, adapting to various social, academic, and professional contexts.  
The goal is for students to become competent and versatile communicators, capable of addressing a wide range of topics  
and situations in English with confidence and effectiveness.
