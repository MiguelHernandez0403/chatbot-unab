2. Resumen del curso 
The A1 Breakthrough English course is designed for beginners who are just starting to learn English. The course will cover 
basic vocabulary, phrases, and grammar. Students will learn to understand and use simple sentences and expressions in 
everyday situat ions, acquire basic vocabulary, practice simple phrases, and ask and answer questions about personal 
information. They will also develop listening skills, practice speaking in short and simple conversations with a focus on 
pronunciation and clarity, and re ad and write simple texts, such as signs, notices, and short messages. By the end of the 
course, students will be able to communicate in basic English using familiar expressions and phrases. They will be able to 
introduce themselves and interact in a simple manner with native speakers who are willing to speak slowly and help them.
